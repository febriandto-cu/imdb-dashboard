Business Insights
Drama Dominates Volume
Drama appears in 5,655 titles — the most frequent genre. However, Documentary (7.31 avg) and History (7.31 avg) lead in average quality despite lower volume.
Votes ↔ Rating Correlation
Strong positive correlation — titles with more votes tend to score higher. Vote count is the #1 predictive feature in both XGBoost models.
TV Shows Outperform Movies
TV Shows avg rating is 7.76 vs Movies at 6.66. 70.9% of TV Shows reach the ≥7.5 "premium" threshold vs only 21.5% of Movies.
Survivorship Bias: Pre-2000s
1920s–1960s titles avg above 7.5 due to survivorship bias — only classics survive. Modern content (2010s–2020s) shows wider quality variance at 6.7–6.8 avg.
numVotes: Top Predictive Feature
XGBoost identified vote count as the dominant feature. log_votes and log_rank together explain the majority of rating variance in both regression and classification.
Premium Content Threshold: 7.5
Only 29.1% of all titles score ≥7.5. Classifier achieves 83.2% accuracy identifying this "Premium Content" tier — 82.7% validated via 5-Fold CV.
