Business Insights
Top Genre: Drama dominates
Drama is the most frequent genre, followed by Comedy and Action. Documentary & History lead in average rating (>7.5).
Votes ↔ Rating Correlation
Strong positive correlation — titles with more votes tend to score higher. Popularity drives credibility.
TV Shows outperform Movies
TV Shows have a slightly higher median IMDb rating than Movies despite lower volume (13.3% vs 84.2%).
Survivorship Bias: Pre-2000s
Titles from 1960s–1990s show higher average ratings due to survivorship bias — only the best-remembered survive.
Feature: numVotes most important
XGBoost identified vote count as the top predictive feature for both regression and classification tasks.
Content Quality Threshold: 7.5
Only ~17% of titles score ≥7.5. Classifier achieves 83% accuracy in identifying this "Premium Content" tier.
